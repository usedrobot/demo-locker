A private locker for your rough mixes. Drop in the demo, send your band the link, and their notes land on the waveform at the second they mean. You host it. You keep the files.

get one

You don't need a terminal. Paste this into Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor:

Set up Demo Locker for me — read https://github.com/usedrobot/demo-locker/blob/main/AGENTS.md and follow it.

It reads the project's own setup runbook and does the rest — asking you to sign in to Cloudflare when it needs to.

what you'll need

  • A Cloudflare account. Free tier is enough.
  • A card on file. Cloudflare requires one to switch on R2 file storage, even though the free allowance covers a band's worth of mixes.
  • A browser, for one click. The assistant will ask you to approve its access to your Cloudflare account.
  • About ten minutes, most of it waiting.

what you get

  • Rough mixes, streamed as you uploaded them. No transcode, no quality loss — a 24-bit WAV comes back a 24-bit WAV.
  • Comments pinned to the waveform. "The snare at 1:42" is a marker at 1:42, not a sentence someone has to decode.
  • Playlists you can re-order. Drag tracks around until the sequence works, then listen straight through.
  • Private links. Send a mix to one person, revoke it later.
  • A player you can embed anywhere. Mark a playlist public and drop it into your own site with one script tag.

or do it yourself

If you'd rather drive:

npx demo-locker

A wizard that asks where it should run — Cloudflare, or Docker on your own machine — and where your files should live, then sets it up.
