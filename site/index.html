A private locker for your rough mixes. Privately collaborate on track listings, add notes to mixes and playlists, and then feel free to share public versions of songs on the frontend of your website. All hosted by you. No streaming terms, no starting and stopping between files, no licensing, no bs, just tunes.

Ask your agent:

Set up Demo Locker for me — read https://raw.githubusercontent.com/usedrobot/demo-locker/main/AGENTS.md and follow it.

Or use our install wizard:

npx demo-locker

Or just run the container yourself:

docker run -d -v demolocker:/data -p 3001:3001 ghcr.io/usedrobot/demo-locker:latest

Multi-arch image, runs on a Pi. Everything lives in one volume: embedded SQLite, your audio on local disk, no external services required. Point it at S3 or R2 instead if you'd rather. See self-hosting, host your music and upgrading.

what to bring with you

  • We recommend one of the following:
    • A Cloudflare account with an R2 bucket, if you want it to live on the internet, or
    • an old machine or a Raspberry Pi with Docker on it, if you'd rather it live on your own shelf.
  • Your demos.
  • Some friends.
